## Problem Statement
You are given an array nums consisting of positive integers and an integer k.
Your task is to find the length of the longest contiguous subarray whose sum is exactly equal to k.


## Approach
-Initialize two pointers l (left) and r (right) at index 0
-Maintain a variable sum for the current window sum
-Expand the window by moving r and add nums[r] to sum
-If sum > k, shrink the window from the left until sum ≤ k
-If sum == k, update the maximum subarray length
-Continue until the end of the array


## Complexity
-Time Complexity: O(n)
-Space Complexity: O(1)
